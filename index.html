About this galaxy

A seeded spiral disc with a central bulge and sparse halo. Stars follow authored circular orbits; differential rotation gradually winds the arms. Set shear to zero for a lasting spiral pattern. This is a kinematic visualization, not a gravitational N-body solver. Logical stars identify a sampled population; only the rendered count is drawn.

Descended from VORTEX’s bounded particle architecture. Captures and settings stay on your device.
